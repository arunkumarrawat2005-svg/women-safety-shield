TIER 1 • STEP 1 Immediate Access

Basic User

Granted immediately upon basic registration (Name, Email, Phone, Password).

PERMITTED (Seeking Protection):
  • Trigger One-Tap SOS emergency distress beacon.
  • Live Google Maps location sharing during emergencies.
  • AI Safe Routes & well-lit corridor navigation.
  • Safe Journey Mode with continuous deviation monitoring.
  • Add and manage personal trusted emergency contacts.
  • View nearby available helpers and safe havens on map.
  • Request Safe Walk escort accompaniment from verified guardians.
  • Receive automated emergency alerts and 112 police dispatch.
RESTRICTIONS (Cannot Assist Others):
  • Cannot accept or respond to another citizen's SOS.
  • Cannot join the active guardian / responder pool.
  • Cannot toggle active responder availability.
  • Cannot accompany or accept safe walk escort requests.
  • Cannot access victim personal information or private dossiers.
  • Cannot represent themselves as a verified community helper.
TIER 2 • STEP 2 & 3 Admin Approved

Verified Community Helper

Granted after KYC Identity Verification, 6-Question Assessment & Admin Approval.

Basic User → KYC Document → Safety Assessment → Admin Review → Verified Helper
COMMUNITY HELPER PERMISSIONS:
  • Verified Local Citizen Helper: Receives nearby SOS alerts in 3km radius.
  • Verified Community Guardian: Toggles live availability & accepts emergencies.
  • Verified Organization Member: Campus/NGO safe havens & monitored shelters.
  • Verified Security/Support Personnel: On-arrival crisis protocol & escort coordination.
  • Need-to-Know Emergency Access: Dispatched location & status for safe intervention.
Strict Data Minimization: Verified helpers receive only the essential coordinates, status, and assistance note required to execute assistance. Victim private dossiers and contact information remain shielded.

Important Privacy Rule: Data Minimization

Being an approved community helper does not grant open surveillance or access to citizens' personal information. The platform enforces data minimization at the API, database, and template layers:

Need-to-Know Location Responders receive the incident location necessary for navigation. Home addresses and unrelated location history are never exposed.
Mutual Identity Reveal Victim and responder names are masked during the incident. Mutual identification is exchanged only upon explicit bilateral consent.
Tamper-Proof Audit Logs Every inquiry, status change, and dispatch event is cryptographically timestamped and logged for Good Samaritan legal protection.
