Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Women Safety Shield Platform

OUR CORE PRIVACY COMMITMENT: Women Safety Shield adheres strictly to purposeful data minimization. Your live location is never tracked continuously or monetized. Telemetry is collected and broadcast strictly during an active emergency SOS distress event or when explicitly requesting a safe escort.

1. Information We Collect

To operate reliable emergency response coordination, we collect the following limited categories of information:

  • Account Details: Full name, verified mobile phone number, email address, and emergency contact list.
  • Identity Verification Records: Government photo ID (Aadhaar or national identification) and live camera verification for community responders, reviewed under administrative oversight.
  • Distress Telemetry: Precise latitude, longitude, speed, battery status, and timestamp, captured strictly during an SOS distress event.
  • Community Hazard Reports: Crowd-sourced safety reports (e.g., poor street lighting, desolate roads) submitted voluntarily to assist route safety assessments.

2. How Telemetry Is Handled During SOS Events

When an SOS alarm is triggered via the panic button, voice command, or emergency shake gesture, your coordinates are transmitted over encrypted TLS channels to:

  • Your pre-configured Trusted Circle members.
  • Aadhaar-verified local community responders located within the active emergency perimeter.
  • Designated security desk officers at nearby participating institutions (colleges, hospitals, tech parks).
  • Official emergency police dispatch integration (112 ERSS).

3. Phone Number Masking & Identity Protection

To protect personal privacy and prevent unsolicited contact, direct telephone numbers between citizens and bystander responders are masked through a privacy relay. Responders see only authorized emergency details necessary to render physical assistance. Full identity unmasking requires mutual consent or lawful administrative police request.

4. Cryptographic Incident Record Preservation

Each distress incident creates an immutable, SHA-256 hashed digital incident certificate. This record documents dispatch timestamps, responder check-ins, and geolocation breadcrumbs. These records are securely preserved to assist official investigations and FIR registration upon victim authorization.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

Users maintain the right to view, update, or request deletion of their profile data at any time from the account settings. Emergency incident records required for statutory police handoff are retained in compliance with applicable public safety laws.

6. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

If you have questions regarding data privacy, security practices, or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact our administrative desk at security@womensafetyshield.org.
