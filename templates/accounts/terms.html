Terms of Service & Safety Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Women Safety Shield Platform

CRITICAL EMERGENCY NOTICE: Women Safety Shield is an auxiliary peer-to-peer community safety and trusted contact alerting network. It is NOT an official government replacement for dialing national emergency services. In any life-threatening crisis, always call Police 112 or Women Helpline 1091 directly.

1. Location & Telemetry Data Consent

By registering and using Women Safety Shield, you explicitly consent to the collection and transmission of your geolocation coordinates during an SOS distress event. Coordinates are shared strictly with your designated Trusted Circle, verified local community responders, and platform security administrators for emergency assistance.

2. Community Responder Verification & Code of Conduct

Citizens registering as Local Residents or Community Helpers undergo identity verification (Aadhaar / Official ID and address validation). Helpers agree to act responsibly, preserve victim dignity, and render immediate bystander aid without intimidation or exploitation. Platform abuse or false claims will lead to immediate account termination and reporting to cyber authorities.

3. Emergency Dispatch & Notification Escrow

When SOS is triggered, the platform attempts parallel delivery via web push notifications, in-app radar broadcasts, and telecom SMS/call escalation (subject to carrier availability). Network latency or mobile device settings may impact transmission speeds. Users acknowledge that maintaining battery and cellular reception is essential for live tracking.

4. Privacy, Cryptography & Audit Logs

All distress events, coordinates, and responder acknowledgments generate server-side tamper-proof Incident Records with cryptographic SHA-256 signatures. These logs are preserved to support legal reporting, FIR assistance, and police investigation upon victim authorization.

5. Safe Routes & Navigation Advisory

Safety scores and lighting assessments are aggregated from crowd-sourced reports, open-street-map metadata, and verified responder density. They are intended as informational indicators. Always remain alert and use primary commercial roads during late-night transit.
