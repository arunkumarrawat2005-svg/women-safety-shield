6-Question Citizen Safety & Awareness Assessment

During identity onboarding, users complete 6 standard Likert-scale statements measuring civic responsibility, willingness to assist safely, women's safety awareness, and emergency preparedness.

Ethical AI Notice: Our AI assessment serves strictly as an orientation signal for human administrators. It never attempts to diagnose mental health, morality, or criminality, nor does it make automated rejection decisions. Human administrators retain final authority.
